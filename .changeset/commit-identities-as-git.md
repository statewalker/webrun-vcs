**Commit, tag and stash identities as native git resolves them.**

- **vcs-commands:** one resolver for every commit and tag the porcelain makes: `resolveAuthorIdent(environment, config)` and `resolveCommitterIdent(environment, config)` follow git's `ident.c`. The name and email come from `GIT_AUTHOR_*` / `GIT_COMMITTER_*` (the host environment), then `author.name` / `author.email` or `committer.name` / `committer.email`, then `user.name` / `user.email`, then `$EMAIL` for the email, with git's crud trimmed. The date is `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE` / `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE`, else the clock in the local timezone offset. With `user.useConfigOnly`, a commit or annotated tag that the configuration names nobody for is refused with git's message (`IdentityUnknownError`). When nothing names anyone, the command's other, explicitly given identity is used, else `webrun-vcs <vcs@localhost>` (the ref stores' reflog identity). No commit is made by `Unknown` any more. `parseGitDate` reads every common form git's `parse_date` reads: raw `<seconds> <+-HHMM>`, `@<seconds>`, seconds alone (in the local offset of that time), RFC 2822, ISO 8601 (with an offset, `Z`, a zone name, or none, which means local time), git's own format, `YYYY.MM.DD`, `MM/DD/YYYY`. ISO and RFC dates keep their offset. An unreadable date is refused with `fatal: invalid date format: <date>` (`InvalidDateError`).
  - The per-command semantics follow git. **commit:** the author comes from `setAuthor` / `setAuthorIdent` or the resolver. `--amend`, and the commit that concludes a cherry-pick, keep the earlier author unless you call the new `setResetAuthor(true)`. `setAuthor(name, email)` replaces only the name and email (its date is the kept author's, else `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE` or now). The committer always comes from the resolver; before, it was the author. **merge (and the merge commit of `pull`):** the author and the committer each come from the resolver. **cherry-pick:** the picked commit's author is kept. **revert:** the resolver gives the author. **rebase picks:** the original author is kept, and the resolver gives the committer. `RebaseCommand.setCommitterDateIsAuthorDate` and `setIgnoreDate` give git's `--committer-date-is-author-date` and `--ignore-date`; both are kept in `rebase-merge/` across a stop and imply a forced rebase. **stash:** the author and the committer each come from the resolver, unless `setAuthor` is given. **tag -a:** the tagger comes from the committer resolver.
  - `GitCommand` gains `resolveAuthor`, `resolveCommitter`, `datedIdent` and `clock` for subclasses. `committerIdent` (reflog lines) now also reads `committer.*`.
- **vcs-working-tree:** `GitHostEnvironment.author` carries git's author variables, and `defaultEmail` carries `$EMAIL`.
- **vcs-utils-node:** `createNodeGitHostEnvironment` reads `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE` and `EMAIL`.
