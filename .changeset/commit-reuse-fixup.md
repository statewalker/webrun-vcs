`git commit -C/-c` and `--fixup`/`--squash` (statewalker/statewalker-root#292). `CommitCommand.setReuseMessage(commit)` (`-C`) and `setReeditMessage(commit)` (`-c`, through `setEditor`) reuse a commit's message and its author, name, email and date (`setResetAuthor` makes a new author, `setAuthor` replaces the name and email). `setFixup("<commit>")` makes `fixup! <subject>` (then the `-m` text), `setFixup("amend:<commit>")` makes `amend! <subject>` followed by the commit's message (its body when it is an `amend!` commit) for the editor, and `setFixup("reword:<commit>")` does the same committing HEAD's tree; `setSquash(commit)` puts `squash! <subject>` before the message. The refusals are git's, with its texts and exit codes: `-m` with `-C`/`-c` or `--fixup=amend:`/`reword:`, `-C`/`-c`/`--fixup` together, `--fixup` with `--squash`, an unknown `--fixup` suboption, a revision that names no commit (`could not lookup commit`), `reword:` with paths, `-a`, `-i` or during a merge or cherry-pick, and an `amend!` message without a body. Such commits fold under `rebase -i --autosquash`, native or ours. A template (`-t`, `commit.template`) is now dropped when `-m`, `-C`, `-c` or `--fixup` is given, as git drops it.
