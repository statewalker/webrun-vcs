`git pull` follows builtin/pull.c (statewalker/statewalker-root#334): it fetches as `git fetch [<remote> [<refspec>...]]` (the remote's configured refspecs when none is given, `.` and paths included) and merges, rebases onto or pulls into the void the `FETCH_HEAD` entries marked for merge, octopus included (`setRefSpecs`). git's refusals come with it: unmerged files and an unconcluded merge before the fetch, `die_no_merge_candidates`, several branches into an empty head or a rebase, `--ff-only` on diverged branches, and divergent branches with neither `pull.rebase` nor `pull.ff` nor an option ("Need to specify how to reconcile divergent branches."); `pull.ff` is read. The merge options pull passes on are new: `setSquash`, `setCommit`, `setEdit`/`setEditor`, `setLog`, `setSignoff`, `setCleanupMode`, `setNoVerify`, `setAllowUnrelatedHistories`, `setStrategyOptions` (`-X`, to the rebase too); and the fetch's: `setAll`, `setPrune`, `setDepth`, `setDeepen`, `setUnshallow`, `setForce`, `setDryRun`. `MergeCommand.setEdit`/`setEditor` run the editor over MERGE_MSG as git's prepare_to_commit, `FetchCommand.setDeepen` is `--deepen=<n>`. `PullCommand.setRecurseSubmodules(true)` refuses until submodule fetching lands.
