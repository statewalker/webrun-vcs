**`git.gc()` now does `git gc`'s whole housekeeping, in git's order, and gives the same results as native git.**

- **vcs-commands:** `GarbageCollectCommand` used to pack refs and prune worktrees only. Over a files repository it now runs the store's `gc()`: `pack-refs --all --prune` (`gc.packRefs`, `notbare` included), `reflog expire --all`, `worktree prune` and `rerere gc` (before the object step as git 2.5x, after it under 2.43 compatibility), the repack (`--cruft` per `gc.cruftPacks`, `-A`, or `-a` for `--prune=now`; `--keep-largest-pack`/`gc.bigPackThreshold`; `--aggressive` with `gc.aggressiveDepth`/`gc.aggressiveWindow`; a bitmap in a bare repository), `update-server-info`, `prune --expire` with `prune_shallow`, and the commit-graph. New setters: `setPrune(date | "now" | "never" | false)`, `setCruft`, `setMaxCruftSize`, `setKeepLargestPack`, `setQuiet`; `setAggressive` and `setExpire` now act. `setAuto(true)` runs git's incremental repack (`repack -d -l --no-write-bitmap-index`) unless there are more packs than `gc.autoPackLimit`, then a full one, and adds git's "too many unreachable loose objects" warning. The result's `objectsRemoved` and `bytesFreed` are now measured (the objects directory before and after; `bytesFreed` is negative when gc grew it), `warnings` and `details` (the store's `GcResult`) are new, and `autoSkipped` may be `"running"` (another gc holds `gc.pid`; without `--auto` the command throws, as git dies).
- **vcs-store-files, as git:** pseudo-refs (`ORIG_HEAD`, `FETCH_HEAD`, `MERGE_HEAD`, `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, `MERGE_AUTOSTASH`, ...) are no longer reachability roots (`git rev-list --all` never lists them): what only they name becomes a cruft object, or goes with `--prune=now`. Prune keeps what `rebase-apply/`/`rebase-merge/` `autostash` and `orig-head` name (git 2.44+, `ReachabilityRoots.rebaseFiles`, `rebaseFileRoots`). Prune walks from recent packed objects too (a cruft pack's `.mtimes`, a pack's time) and drops the shallow commits nothing reaches. Temporaries (`tmp_obj_*`, `objects/pack/tmp_*`) expire with the prune expiry. A lone `.idx` and its sibling files are removed (`clean_pack_garbage`). Reflog expiry rewrites every reflog as git does (`<tz>\t<message>`), `pack-refs` writes `packed-refs` even with nothing to pack.
- **vcs-store-files, new:** `GcOptions.prune`, `aggressive`, `keepLargestPack`, `auto`, `compatibility`, `packRefsStep`, `beforeObjects`/`afterObjects`, `rerereGc`; `GcResult.autoRepack`, `keptLargePacks`, `rerere`, `shallowPruned`, `removedPackGarbage`, `storage`, `warnings`; `RepackOptions.keepPacks` (`--keep-pack`) and `updateServerInfo`; `updateServerInfo()` (`git update-server-info`, which `repack()` now runs as git's repack does) and `rerereGc()` (`git rerere gc`).
